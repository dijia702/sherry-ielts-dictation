# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese IELTS learners practising high-frequency listening vocabulary on desktop and mobile, usually in focused, repeated study sessions.

## Product Purpose

Provide a local-first dictation workflow that makes listening, spelling, checking answers, reviewing mistakes, and tracking progress fast and reliable.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, and friendly. The interface should feel supportive without becoming playful enough to distract from listening practice.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style hero layouts, decorative card stacks, loud gamification, excessive animation, and dense statistics that compete with the current word.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current listening task visually dominant.
- Make every repeated action predictable and keyboard-friendly.
- Use feedback that is immediate, warm, and unambiguous.
- Preserve progress locally and make recovery explicit.
- Keep desktop and mobile workflows equally complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain clear focus states, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation, semantic success and error text, sufficient contrast, stable control sizes, and reduced-motion support.
